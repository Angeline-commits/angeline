Angeline Gautami Fernando

Associate Professor of Marketing
Great Lakes Institute of Management, Chennai

My primary research interest is digital marketing, specifically understanding online consumer behaviour and how consumers engage with technology, and using analytics to gain consumer intelligence from social media. Most of my research questions are framed in these contexts. I teach marketing and analytics at Great Lakes Institute of Management, Chennai.
