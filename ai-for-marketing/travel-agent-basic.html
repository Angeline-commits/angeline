Tool name: available_destinations

Description: This tool returns a string containing information about the destinations available for booking based on geography and the best time and month to visit. The different types of destination categories are Beach, Mountain ,Wildlife, Desert Heritage, Spiritual and Backwater. It can answer questions such as: "What are the trending beach destinations?" or "What is the best month to visit Goa?"
